Why not just use WhatsApp Business?

Because WhatsApp Business doesn't know your schedule, your no-show rate, or your prices.

WhatsApp Business is free and it sends auto-replies. It can't check a doctor's real calendar, can't tell a patient what a filling costs, and can't tell the owner whether Tuesdays are quietly losing money to no-shows. That's the gap Recall fills.

Always-on chatbot

Answers "Hi" at 11 PM the same way it does at 11 AM a full booking conversation, not a canned auto-reply.

Answers the questions you repeat all day

Hours, insurance, pricing, aftercare, first-time visits you write the answer once from Settings, and the bot gives it correctly, or tells the patient to call rather than guessing.

Live availability, per doctor

Set each dentist's working days and hours once the bot only ever offers a slot that actually exists, and suggests the next open day when one's full.

Confirm, cancel, or mark a no-show

New bookings land as "pending" until staff confirms. When a patient doesn't show, mark it that's the number the owner needs to see, not just guess at.

A team, not one shared password

Give the front desk and each dentist their own login instead of everyone sharing "admin." Owner-only controls stay owner-only.

Reports an owner actually reads

Bookings over time, no-show rate, and which doctor is busiest for the same login that used to just see a list of appointments.

Setup

Four steps to your first WhatsApp booking.

A real installation timeline, in the order it actually happens not marketing steps.

Step 1

Connect your WhatsApp number

Paste your Twilio or Meta WhatsApp credentials into Clinic Settings yourself patients still message the number they already have saved.

Step 2

Add your doctors

Enter each dentist's working days and hours. Recall turns that straight into bookable slots.

Step 3

Teach the bot your answers

Write your hours, pricing, insurance policy, and aftercare notes once the bot uses exactly that wording, nothing invented.

Step 4

Go live

Patients start booking immediately. Staff confirms, cancels, or marks a no-show from the dashboard as it comes in.
